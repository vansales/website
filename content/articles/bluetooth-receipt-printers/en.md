On a cash van route, the sale isn't finished when the customer says yes — it's finished when the bill is printed, the goods are handed over, and the cash is counted. A mobile printer turns all three into one moment at the shop counter. No going back to the office to issue an invoice, no hand-written slips to reconcile later, no arguments about what was delivered.

Pair a small **Bluetooth thermal printer** with the Vansales app on the rep's phone and every order prints on the spot: an itemised bill, a delivery note, or a tax invoice — straight from the same order the rep just keyed in. Here's how to think about the hardware, and the three printers we've tested in the field.

## Why print in the field at all?

- **You get paid faster.** A bill in hand is a bill that gets settled. Cash-on-delivery is far easier to collect when the customer can see exactly what they owe.
- **Fewer disputes.** The printout is the record. What was ordered, what was delivered, and the price are all on paper before the rep drives away.
- **You look professional.** A clean printed receipt with your logo beats a torn page from a carbon book — especially in traditional trade where trust is the whole relationship.
- **The numbers stay clean.** Because the receipt prints from the order in Vansales, your books and the shop's copy always match. Nothing is re-keyed.

## What to look for

Not every printer is built for a hot truck and a full day on the road. Before you buy, check:

- **Paper width — 58 mm or 80 mm.** 58 mm is compact and cheap on paper; 80 mm fits more columns and reads better for longer bills. Most field teams are happy with 58 mm; pick 80 mm if your invoices are detailed.
- **Battery life.** You want a full route on one charge. Look for a battery that lasts a day and a spare or a car charger for long routes.
- **Build quality.** Field printers get dropped, sat on, and rained on. A rubberised, drop-rated body pays for itself.
- **Bluetooth pairing.** It should reconnect to the rep's phone automatically each morning without fuss. Test this before you roll out a fleet.
- **Print speed and paper cost.** Thermal printing needs no ink or ribbon — just rolls of thermal paper, which are cheap and easy to find. Keep spares on the van.

## Setup is quick

Charge the printer, load a roll of thermal paper, turn on Bluetooth on the rep's phone, and pair. In the Vansales app, select the printer once and it's remembered. From then on, finishing an order and tapping print puts a bill in the customer's hand in seconds.
